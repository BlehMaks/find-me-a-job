# Germany: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

Start with a concise reverse-chronological industry CV, tailored required letter and requested supporting evidence. A textual single-column layout preserves readable extraction. Do not assume a photo, birth date, marital status or full street address is necessary; confirm actual requirements and the person's disclosure choice. Keep supporting certificates/references separate when the ATS has distinct fields.

Use German or English according to the advertisement and confirmed proficiency. Regulated qualification and work-authorization requirements need current authoritative verification. Do not convert grades or claim recognized equivalence without the permitted method and inputs.

Search official employer/institution portals, the Federal Employment Agency and approved specialist/public-opening resources. LinkedIn and authorized email alerts supplement discovery. Academic applications may emphasize research fit, publications, teaching or host relationships rather than industry brevity.
