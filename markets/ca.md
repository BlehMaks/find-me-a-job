# Canada: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

Government Job Bank recommends concise relevant accomplishments, generally within two pages, and advises against a photograph. Use that as an industry starting point, with no unnecessary personal characteristics. Academic, government and specific programme instructions may require different length or evidence.

Keep a master evidence base and tailor the actual application. State supported contribution and results clearly; missing metrics do not justify invented numbers. Confirm language, province/location, professional registration and work-authorization requirements from the actual posting and authoritative sources.

Use official employer/institution portals and Job Bank alongside approved professional sources and alerts. Some portals ask detailed screening questions; respond to the exact criteria with confirmed examples rather than assuming the CV answers them. Preserve distinct CV/letter/supporting upload fields and the form's accepted files.
