# United Kingdom: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

Start with a relevant CV using clear contact, experience, education and skills sections; change emphasis for the role and career stage. Omit unnecessary age, marital-status and nationality details. Do not include referee contact information by default. Requested supporting statements may need explicit criterion-by-criterion evidence rather than a short cover letter.

Academic routes differ: research, teaching, service, publications and funding can justify longer structured CVs and separate statements. Clearly distinguish published from submitted/in-preparation work. Some schemes use a narrative contribution format such as UKRI R4RI; use the actual call's modules and limits rather than forcing an industry template.

Search official employer/university portals, professional sources and approved people/group/funder leads. Confirm current eligibility, sponsorship and deadlines; no general profile substitutes for the live specification.
