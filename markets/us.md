# United States: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

For ordinary industry roles begin with a simple achievement-focused resume, standard section names and a readable chronology. Choose length for relevant evidence and actual instructions rather than imposing one page universally. A combination structure can emphasize transferable skills while retaining work history.

CareerOneStop recommends clear formatting and standard headings. It is a useful industry guide, not an academic or federal application rule. Academic CVs, grants and public-sector portals can require different scope, documents and exact eligibility details; inspect the live call. Keep proposed research and incomplete publications accurately labelled.

Discover via official employers/institutions, approved specialist boards and LinkedIn/authorized alerts where relevant. Confirm work authorization/sponsorship questions independently and retain exact scope; never infer eligibility or turn an answer to one legal declaration into a blanket answer.
