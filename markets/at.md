# Austria: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

AMS describes a conventional packet of letter, CV and relevant evidence, with optional extra sheets. Treat that as a starting convention; ATS fields and the advertisement determine the actual package. Avoid automatically combining everything when separate uploads are requested. A photo is a personal option, not a product requirement.

A concise industry CV can often use one or two pages when evidence supports it. Academic/creative applications follow their institution or call. Confirm application language, title conventions and specialist qualification requirements. Never insert personal information merely because an older template contains it.

Discover through official employer/institution sites, AMS resources and approved professional or discipline-specific sources, using LinkedIn/authorized alerts where useful. Verify live eligibility, location, deadline and requested documents before tailoring.
