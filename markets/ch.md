# Switzerland: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

Use a clear relevant chronology and employer-specific package. Choose the language from the actual advertisement and person's confirmed ability; Switzerland has multiple language markets. Do not assume every employer expects the same photo, reference or personal-detail convention.

ETH's Welcome Center points to a Career Center application guide and information about working in Switzerland. This is a useful institutional entry point, not a rule governing all Swiss employers. Read the institution's own submission instructions; academic and research applications may require publications, statements and referees beyond an industry CV.

Search official employer/institution vacancies and approved specialist sources. Verify work authorization, location and any qualification requirements from current official sources rather than inferring eligibility from nationality or another country's permission. Preserve requested supporting-document field boundaries.
