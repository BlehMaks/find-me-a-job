# France: starter profile

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Guidance, not universal requirements or immigration/legal advice. Recheck sources and actual call before campaign activation; institution/discipline and explicit call rules override these defaults.

Choose a concise CV in the advertisement's language with evidence ordered for the target work. France Travail discusses when two pages can serve substantial varied experience; no universal one-page rule follows. A clear single-column textual structure is a useful ATS-safe starting point, while creative portfolios stay separate unless the call requests otherwise.

Prepare a tailored required motivation letter/email; optional letters follow campaign policy. Keep original qualification names/scales clear and do not invent a French equivalence. Academic, artistic, public-sector and funding calls may prescribe distinct documents or formal routes; follow those over general advice.

Discover via official employer/institution sites, France Travail and approved professional/discipline sources, supplemented by LinkedIn or authorized alerts. Confirm live duties, language, location, eligibility and deadlines before tailoring. Do not carry another market's personal-data habits into the profile automatically.
