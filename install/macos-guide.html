Two steps to get started.

1. Copy the app to Applications.

In the disk window, drag Find Me a Job Setup onto the Applications folder. Wait for copying to finish.

2. Open the copied app.

Open Applications in Finder and double-click Find Me a Job Setup. You can then eject the Find Me a Job Setup disk.

What happens next?

The wizard asks you to choose Codex or Claude, review the software it needs, and select separate folders for the product and your private data. Start with Check & review plan, then choose Install / retry when ready.

Once setup and PDF checks pass, use Copy first message. Open the source folder shown by the wizard in your agent app and paste that message. Your agent will ask for access to the separate private folder.

If macOS blocks opening: this is an unsigned private pilot. Verify the download with the maintainer before proceeding. Do not disable macOS security protections globally. A subscription is not included or purchased during setup; sign in to your chosen agent and confirm its plan separately.
