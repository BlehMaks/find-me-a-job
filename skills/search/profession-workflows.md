# Profession-specific opportunity pathways

## Academic/research

Interview about topic, methods, career stage, institution/geography, teaching, funding eligibility and preferred search process. Search current group/person work, department vacancies, institutional career pages, fellowship/program calls and relevant funder schemes. A person/topic match is a lead, not an open funded position; verify available route, eligibility, host requirements and deadline. Use current publications/group pages accurately and distinguish author contributions.

For approved direct outreach, explain a specific intellectual connection and the person's supported contribution, then ask a clear limited question about possible opportunities. Use `prompts/communication.md`; do not claim a relationship or invent knowledge of papers. Where a formal call is required, outreach complements rather than replaces it.

Research statements, teaching statements, publication lists and narrative contribution formats may matter more than a short industry CV. Actual scheme requirements override general advice. Label publication status and future work. For grants/programs use the complete requirement matrix and substantive approval in `prompts/letters-and-statements.md`; the assistant may submit the approved package under the agreed procedure.

Sources reviewed 2026-10-20: [Oxford academic applications](https://www.ox.ac.uk/careers/careers-guidance/job-search-and-applications/writing-applications/academic-applications), [UKRI narrative R4RI](https://www.ukri.org/apply-for-funding/develop-your-application/resume-for-research-and-innovation-r4ri-guidance/). These are institution/scheme examples, not rules for all academia.

## Creative and portfolio work

Interview about discipline, target briefs, medium, credited role, collaboration and rights. Search official employer opportunities, studios/institutions, commissions/residencies and discipline-relevant calls approved in the source strategy. Distinguish employment, freelance commission, competition and unpaid opportunity; paid entry/new obligations need human approval.

Select portfolio pieces that prove the requested craft, explain personal role and outcome, and verify permission to share. Preserve credits and confidentiality; no confidential client work or imagery is uploaded without authorization. Keep the parseable CV separate from the portfolio unless the call requires another arrangement. Prepare tailored artistic/project statements when required; verify asset size/type/count and actual links before sending.

## Industry and other highly qualified professions

Read duties rather than assuming engineering patterns apply. Build evidence around the person's actual methods, clients/stakeholders, specialist judgment and outcomes. Use professional associations, specialist boards, employer sites and relevant recruiters within the approved strategy. Check regulated qualifications and eligibility from authoritative sources where material. An adjacent occupation may be an honest transfer, not a substitute for a legally required qualification.
