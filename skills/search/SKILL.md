# Search and screening

Require approved campaign briefing and general CV before campaign activation. Read current policy, completed catalogue coverage, exclusions and unresolved questions. Do not repeat an entire company sweep because a summary is stale. Record source coverage separately from pending applications.

## Discovery

Use public openings and LinkedIn/authorized mailbox alerts as initial discovery channels when relevant. Set roughly three to five approved title alerts with agreed location, language and cadence, if the person wants them. Use people/groups/programs/calls for fields where these are more effective (`profession-workflows.md`). Research other useful resources within approved classes. Capture canonical employer/institution URL, requisition/call ID, source URL, discovered time and live requirements. Prefer the official employer/institution/ATS route; LinkedIn is a discovery source, not a reason to duplicate a formal application.

Read full duties for plausible matches. A senior/lead title can be hands-on; a familiar title can hide unsuitable duties. Resolve search-card vs posting location, remote-country eligibility, mandatory language/qualifications and current opening status. Reject obvious hard-limit violations early; do not spend a full tailoring cycle first. Retain direct evidence, honest transfer, gap and unknown classifications, not made-up ATS percentages.

## Identity and prioritization

Canonical employer+requisition/call identity plus URL aliases prevents multi-city/cross-board duplicates. A second distinct requisition at one employer can be a separate opportunity. Ambiguous identity needs reconciliation before sending. Do not use row counts or employer name alone as proof of unique applications.

Queue high-value viable opportunities first using approved priorities, deadlines and evidence strength. Check employer/end-client exclusions again before submission. Source capture, screening, document readiness, form state and delivery remain separate. A blocked login does not make the vacancy unsuitable; share that blocker only over its observed scope and continue independent applications.

## Outputs

Keep an inspectable opportunity ledger with screening reasons, matched evidence/gaps, source/confidence, deadline/timezone, official application route and next step. Report actual newly observed submissions separately from recovered receipts and prepared-but-blocked items. Follow `skills/applications/SKILL.md` for execution and `scheduling.md` for recurring work.

LinkedIn account ownership does not imply automation permission or no restriction risk. Use only the approved current integration within host and platform constraints; do not evade limits or create replacement accounts after restriction.
