# Fact, claim and wording variants

A source assertion becomes a usable fact when supported and non-conflicting or explicitly confirmed by the person. Keep exact values, precision, source IDs, confirmation, effective dates and qualifiers. Separate needs-confirmation, conflicting and confirmed-absent states. Never interpret lack of evidence as a negative fact.

For each material claim store: fact IDs; safe assertion; context; allowed verbs/metrics; ownership/proficiency/status limits; forbidden implications; language/audience variants; approval and version. Derived documents refer to claim IDs. Every variant retains the same evidence boundary.

Example (entirely synthetic): a researcher confirms that they designed an interview protocol and analysed 18 interviews as part of a team. Safe variants: “Designed the interview protocol and analysed 18 interviews for a collaborative study” or “Developed a qualitative protocol and interpreted interview findings.” Unsafe: “Led the research group,” “published 18 studies,” or an invented impact percentage. Do not present a proposal as a completed study.

Create variants by useful emphasis, not arbitrary synonym spinning:

| Context | Emphasis | Must preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry | Concrete delivery, stakeholder or process outcome | Personal contribution, deployment stage, metric scope |
| Academic | Question, method, contribution, publication status | Authorship, actual findings vs future research |
| Creative | Brief, credited role, craft, audience/client outcome | Rights, collaboration, publication/delivery status |
| Transfer | Shared method/result relevant to adjacent role | Exact requested skill remains a gap if not demonstrated |

Use direct, confident verbs when supported. “Built” may replace vague “helped with” only if the person actually built the object; it does not imply sole ownership. Keep critical skill gaps explicit in matching notes and answer honestly when asked. Unsupported noncritical wording is still not a license to invent facts.

Date audit: current/closed status must be confirmed, dates keep their known precision, newest experience normally leads, and subprojects stay under the appropriate parent unless the selected template has a justified alternative. Independent projects and career transitions use the person's confirmed policy; no source candidate's dates or labels are defaults.
