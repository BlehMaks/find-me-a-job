# Interview briefing prompt

Produce a concise personal preparation pack from the role/call, invitation, sent documents, confirmed experience and current primary-source research:

1. Logistics requiring human confirmation: proposed date/timezone, format, people, tasks and deadline. Draft an invitation reply; do not send acceptance autonomously.
2. Employer/group: what it does, relevant current work and why this role exists where supported. Link sources, dates and uncertainty. Avoid generic praise and unsupported interviewer profiling.
3. Fit: strongest three or four evidence-backed connections, actual gaps and likely concerns. Explain candid answers for unknown tools/methods using relevant transfer and a realistic learning approach.
4. Introductions: approximately 30-second and 90-second spoken versions plus cue words. Keep one consistent story with role-specific emphasis; preserve actual titles/dates/contribution.
5. Stories: context, task, personal action, method, result and learning. Include collaboration, difficult decision/failure and strongest achievement where supported. Distinguish team result from personal role; no invented numbers.
6. Likely technical/discipline/behavioural questions with evidence-bounded talking points and questions the candidate can ask about work, expectations and process.
7. Practical preparation: optional rehearsal, requested presentation/portfolio and precise missing facts. Save candidate corrections for later use.

Use approved voice, accessible spoken language and Humanizer when it improves wording. Preparation does not certify ability or guarantee an interview outcome.
