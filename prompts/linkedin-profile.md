# Broad professional profile and network setup

Recommend access to the person's LinkedIn profile after the general CV is confirmed; explain that a broad coherent profile can support several approved role families. Ask what the current employer may learn. Access is scoped and optional, not a condition for producing a CV.

Inspect the current profile and prepare headline, About, experience, skills and relevant featured links from confirmed claims. Keep dates, titles, proficiency and publication/status consistent with the general CV. A broad profile foregrounds shared strengths rather than copying one vacancy's keywords. Preserve valid breadth and approved authorial voice. Show material changes before applying them under granted authority; never invent endorsements or credentials.

The person normally sets Open to Work and visibility themselves. Provide concise instructions and record the choice without assuming a public frame. If delegated, explicitly agree public vs recruiter-only visibility first; recruiter-only cannot be represented as guaranteed secrecy. Configure approved alerts around three to five titles where useful.

Agree a practical networking policy by audience and purpose. A recruiter-heavy start or approximately 70% recruiters / 30% relevant peers can be discussed as options, never universal defaults. Choose relevant people, verify prior contacts/pending requests/refusals across channels, and combine relevant roles in one purposeful message. Respect approved caps and platform limits. Do not automatically demand referrals from everyone added.

Use `prompts/communication.md` for text and `skills/humanizer/SKILL.md` for editing. Account/browser permission does not remove platform restrictions: [LinkedIn automated activity](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341543), [Open to Work visibility](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a507508). Recheck current terms and integration capability during setup; never promise unrestricted automated networking.
