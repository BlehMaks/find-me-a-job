# Scoped recurring answers and custom questions

During intake collect common form facts/preferences as applicable: contact/location, work authorization/sponsorship, relocation, travel, languages, compensation expectations, notice/availability, education scales, references, disclosure/conflicts and accessibility needs if voluntarily provided. Do not solicit sensitive information without a concrete need. Do not import another person's answers or default a declaration to “no.”

For each answer save the **exact question or semantic scope**, answer, fact/confirmation references, confirmer/date, campaign/employer applicability, expiry or recalculation rule and superseded version. Similar words do not guarantee the same meaning. Citizenship, work permission, sponsorship, criminal history, family relationships and government service are distinct questions. One confirmed declaration cannot answer unrelated ones.

When a form asks:

1. Read the full wording and choices, including units and period. Find a current matching scoped answer.
2. Reuse only within its scope. For a changed question, unknown material fact or conflict, persist a concrete question with options/context and continue independent work.
3. A confirmed absence is an answer, not a missing handoff. Transferable exposure does not become a requested platform skill. Critical gaps are answered honestly.
4. Compute dynamic dates using the person's confirmed rule, current application date and campaign timezone. Never copy a stale date. Preserve original grading scales; conversion requires authorized method and necessary inputs, never invented official equivalence.
5. Compose custom text from approved claims and motivation, honouring the word/character limit. Humanize and verify exact meaning/qualifiers and form validation.
6. Freeze exact question, answer, policy/fact versions and final form values with the submitted operation. A later correction updates future answers and adds history; it does not rewrite prior submissions.

For managed clients send personal-fact questions through the bound paid channel. Campaign/service/access/cost changes go to the operator. Do not ask passwords, verification codes or sensitive documents in chat.
