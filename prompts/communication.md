# Professional communication and correspondence

Read current authority, audience/purpose policy, prior/pending messages, refusal state, selected contact identity and the actual thread. Use approved voice and Humanizer. Remain professional and slightly more polite than the counterpart. Prefer a short clear purpose and a relevant evidence-backed connection over promotional claims or formulaic praise.

| Audience | Purpose and content |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Relevant role/application, strongest fit, concise request for consideration or review |
| Hiring manager | Specific work/problem, supported contribution, clear interest/question |
| Peer/referral contact | Why this person is relevant, role(s), honest connection and limited referral/process question |
| Researcher/group | Accurate topical connection, personal method/contribution, targeted opportunity/host question |
| Existing contact | Relationship-aware reply; do not repeat background they already know |
| Creative commissioner | Relevant credited work, fit to brief, approved portfolio and concrete request |

These are purpose distinctions, not rigid scripts. A referral contact authorized only for a form is not automatically messageable. Do not invent familiarity, referrals or endorsements. Combine relevant roles in one message when appropriate; check contact history across channels before new outreach. Respect agreed caps/follow-up windows and refusals. A clear no ends the sequence.

Before send verify To/Cc/Bcc and named salutation. Use Reply all only when the conversational purpose requires all recipients; a group thread does not require a group greeting. Write new reply text above and **outside** quoted history, without inherited quote bars. Inspect the final composed message, not only the draft. Keep email subjects/threads and attachments appropriate.

Routine permitted answers may be sent within policy. Appointment confirmation, offer decisions, final compensation/terms, withdrawal, paid steps and new material commitments go to the responsible human with a draft recommendation. Do not treat an invitation as an accepted meeting. Managed service/campaign/access/cost changes go to the operator; personal factual questions may go to the bound client.

For sends, use the same serialized operation, final authority/pause/deduplication gate and unknown-delivery reconciliation as applications. A click without observed evidence is not confirmed delivery. Record recipient, purpose, associated opportunities, exact sent version and observed outcome privately. Do not export correspondence to upstream feedback.
