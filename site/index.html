01 — THE PROCESS

Start with the person.
Build from the evidence.

Bring a few old CVs, your projects or just your story. Your agent asks the questions that a template never will.

  1. 01

    Reconstruct your experience

    Compare earlier CVs. Explain each project in ordinary language. Separate your contribution from the team's, resolve contradictions and find achievements you may have overlooked.

  2. 02

    Find the right expression

    Confirm the facts and wording. Choose a document structure. Calibrate the writing against your own samples, then build a general CV and reusable, evidence-linked variations.

  3. 03

    Agree on the search

    Set markets, desired work, dislikes and firm limits. Research relevant vacancies, groups, people and programmes. Approve a broader search strategy before it starts.

  4. 04

    Apply, correspond, prepare

    Tailor documents and form answers. Check the actual attachments before sending. Keep a journal, resume blocked applications and prepare for invitations using your own experience.

05 — CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Assistance with
room for judgment.

Private workspace, disclosed processing

Personal records stay outside product source and public Git history. Your configured agent and connected services still process the information they need; local storage does not mean offline inference. Credentials stay out of journals and feedback.

You approve the consequential decisions

The agent can apply and correspond within your agreed scope. You retain appointments, offers, final compensation and terms, paid actions and new material commitments. Complex proposals require substantive approval before submission.

Login handoffs stay honest

The agent reuses permitted sessions and saves progress. Tool-required credential, CAPTCHA and agreement confirmations remain human actions. Browser ownership does not remove platform restrictions.

Tested capabilities, without blanket promises

Setup checks the actual environment. Platforms and agent integrations have separate tested, missing and untested states. No guarantee of interviews, offers, universal ATS acceptance or removal of every statistical watermark.

Improvements are your choice

Setup asks before enabling upstream issues or isolated pull requests. Only inspected generic descriptions and synthetic reproductions can be shared. Silence means no sending; personal memory is never a feedback attachment.
